Live

Andhra Pradesh Groundwater Dashboard

A district-level tool for exploring 13 groundwater indicators across all 26 districts, with a 2026 forecast for each. Every district is officially "Safe", yet extraction ranges from 4% to 57%, so the dashboard shows which ones are moving fastest and why.

  • Replaced manual data entry with automated extraction from CGWB PDF reports.
  • Forecasts average linear, polynomial and random-forest models, and show how much the three agree.
  • Random forest plus SHAP explains each district's extraction stage and drives policy recommendations.
  • Covered by The Times of India as an IIM Visakhapatnam public-policy initiative.
Python, Flask, scikit-learn, SHAP, pandas, Plotly, pdfplumber, Linux, Gunicorn, SSL, UFW

Live

NIRF Decision Support System

Explores four years of NIRF Management ranking data (2022–2025, 401 institute-year records, 15 sub-scores) and forecasts each institute's 2026 score and rank.

  • Validated forecasts chronologically (train on earlier years, test on the next) instead of a random split, to avoid leaking future data.
  • Compared Linear, Ridge, PLS, tree ensembles and more, scoring both error (MAE, R²) and ranking quality (Spearman, top-10 overlap).
  • The deployed model is deliberately simple: linear regression on year-to-year score transitions, with rank derived by sorting predicted scores.
Python, Flask, scikit-learn, pandas, Plotly, Gunicorn
